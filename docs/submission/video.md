## Draft narration

Edit it into your own words. Say it slowly.

**Hook.** This is Kamla. She is 62, she has a basic phone, and she cannot climb stairs. She has come to the hospital to see a doctor, and she cannot find the room.

**Three screens.** OPD Saathi has three screens for one hospital. The Patient screen, the Staff dashboard, and the Waiting-room board. They update together.

**Register.** Kamla picks Hindi. The first screen lists emergency signs. If one of them applies, the Emergency button takes her straight to Casualty, and it is on every screen. She turns on "Someone is helping me". Text gets bigger and everything is read aloud. Registration is one question at a time, and we never ask for Aadhaar or ABHA numbers. She gets a slip with a token, her department, the room and a QR code.

**Route.** The app shows the way from the entrance. Avoid stairs is on, so the route uses the lift, even though the stairs are shorter. The steps use landmarks, in Hindi, and she can press Listen.

**Queue.** On the staff side we add 20 sample patients and speed up time. Staff call the next token. The board shows it and announces it aloud. Kamla sees people ahead drop. The estimated wait is people ahead times the recent consultation time, rounded to five minutes, so it always says "about", with a range beside it. When three are ahead she gets an alert. The text message you see is a sample. No real SMS is sent.

**Urgent flag.** If someone needs help now, they press "I need urgent help". The flag pins to the top of the staff dashboard with a sound until staff mark it seen. The app never decides who is urgent. Only qualified staff do.

**Called.** Kamla's screen says it is her turn. She follows the route, and staff mark the consultation done.

**Limits.** To be clear about what this is. It is a prototype on made-up data. There is no server, so the queue lives in the browser and syncs across tabs of one browser. We cut the server and the daily report to finish a complete flow. There is no staff login, no real SMS, and we have not tested it in a real OPD.

**Next.** Next, a real server so queues sync across devices, Scan and Share for registration, Odia as a third language, and a trial in a real OPD to measure the time from slip to room.

## Do not say or show

- Never call a case urgent or non-urgent, and never tell anyone they can wait.
- Do not claim the Render Workflows track, or a feature that is not built. There is no mic input and no live multi-device sync.
- No real names, phone numbers, Aadhaar or ABHA numbers. Use Kamla and made-up numbers.
- Do not show the dashboard's browser tabs or any account, key or ticket.
- Do not say "India averages". Name the hospital if you quote a figure.
