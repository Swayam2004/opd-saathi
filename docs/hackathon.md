## What we submit

1. Public GitHub repository (this repo, made public at submission time).
2. Live working URL.
3. A 5-minute demo video.
4. A short pitch deck (HackCache provides a 7-slide template).

For the Render track, the demo link is the live Render URL.

## How we're judged

Six judges score every submission independently on five criteria, each out of 10, 50 total. Averages decide the ranking.

| Criterion | Out of |
| --- | --- |
| Innovation | 10 |
| Technical Execution | 10 |
| Design and UX | 10 |
| Impact | 10 |
| Presentation | 10 |

The brief's own focus list (ease of registration, language accessibility, inclusive design, queue efficiency, navigation accuracy, emergency handling, technical execution, real-world usefulness) is guidance for what to show.

## Prizes

Cash: 1st Rs 26,999, 2nd Rs 21,000, 3rd Rs 17,000. 1st place also gets 3 paid internships and a McLeod Ganj trip. Every submitting team gets a certificate.

## Render track (bonus, open to every team)

Deploy on Render and use **Render Workflows**. Prizes are Render credits: $500, $300, $100 for the top three, and $50 for every participant. Judges look at innovation, effective use of Workflows, technical implementation, real-world impact and UX. Workflows are beta or limited early access, so confirm access early. Findings are in `docs/research/` once the Render research lands.

## Rules that matter to us

- AI use is allowed and encouraged. We must be able to explain what we built.
- Build during the hackathon. Anything pre-existing must be declared. Generic libraries and boilerplate are fine.
- Use made-up data. No real Aadhaar or ABHA numbers, no real patient data.
- The FAQ doesn't say whether a team may submit twice. We plan for one submission.
